# Pack: report style

For long client deliverables: research reports, readiness assessments, audits.
These are read slowly, forwarded, and quoted back to you. Small sloppiness
costs trust.

Items in R1 and R2 are soft flags. **Hard fail if their total exceeds ten** in
one report. Items in R3 are hard fails on their own.

## R1. Prose rules

- An em-dash in body prose (use your house rule from `config/voice.md`).
- A comma joining three or more clauses in one sentence.
- A contraction outside a quotation.
- More than two bold spans in one paragraph.
- A percent sign in prose, or the spelled-out word in a table (pick one per
  context and hold it).

## R2. Structure rules

- A table with no sentence before or after it saying what to read from it.
- A section that ends on a recap of itself.
- A term, scale or instrument used before it is defined.
- A callout label outside your fixed set (example set: NOTE, REVISED, LIMIT,
  CONFIRMED), or an emoji used as a label.

## R3. Version and sync (hard fail on any one)

- The version and date in the document's change block differ from the cover.
- More than one submission date in the same document.
- A box says "nothing changed" while another box lists a change.
- The same scale or rating cited with different values across the documents
  of one set.

Add your own house rules here. Keep each one testable by reading the text.
